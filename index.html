Stay Safe Online

Five simple habits that protect your accounts and your data.

Passwords Two-step login Phishing Updates Public Wi-Fi

1. Use strong, different passwords

A strong password is long and hard to guess. Never use the same password on two websites. If one website is hacked, attackers try that password everywhere else.

  • Use at least 12 characters.
  • Mix words, numbers and symbols.
  • Use a password manager to remember them for you.

2. Turn on two-step login

Two-step login (also called two-factor authentication) asks for a second proof, like a code on your phone, after your password. Even if someone steals your password, they still cannot get in.

3. Watch out for phishing

Phishing is when a fake message pretends to be a real company to trick you into giving your details.

Warning sign What to do
Urgent message ("act now or lose your account") Stop and think. Real companies rarely rush you.
Strange sender address or spelling mistakes Do not click. Delete the message.
Link asking for your password Open the official website yourself instead.

4. Keep your devices updated

Updates fix security holes that attackers know about. Turn on automatic updates for your phone, laptop and apps.

5. Be careful on public Wi-Fi

Anyone on the same public network may be able to watch unprotected traffic. Prefer websites that start with https, and avoid logging in to important accounts on public Wi-Fi.
